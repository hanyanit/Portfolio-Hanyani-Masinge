# Agile Team Charter — Opportunity Connect

## 1. Project Overview

**Project Name:** Opportunity Connect (OppCo)  
**Project Type:** Student Opportunity and Bursary Platform  
**Development Methodology:** Agile  
**Frontend Technology:** Angular  
**Backend Technology:** Python  
**Version Control:** GitHub  
 

### Project Description

Opportunity Connect is a web-based platform designed to connect students with bursaries and other educational opportunities that may be relevant to them.

The platform aims to make it easier for students to discover opportunities by providing information about available opportunities and helping users identify opportunities that match their circumstances and requirements.

The project was developed using Agile principles, allowing the development work to be divided into smaller tasks and progressively improved throughout the development process.

---

## 2. Team Purpose

The purpose of the Opportunity Connect development team was to design, develop and improve a functional platform that addresses the difficulty students experience when searching for relevant bursaries and opportunities.

The team aimed to:

- Develop a user-friendly platform for students.
- Identify and prioritise important system requirements.
- Break large requirements into smaller user stories and development tasks.
- Develop and test functionality incrementally.
- Communicate progress and challenges throughout development.
- Continuously improve the product based on feedback and testing.
- Use version control to manage changes to the application.
- Deliver a functional product that provides value to its intended users.

---

## 3. Team Roles and Responsibilities

The Opportunity Connect project required different responsibilities throughout the development process.

| Role / Responsibility | Description |
|---|---|
| Product Planning | Identifying the purpose of the platform and prioritising important functionality. |
| Frontend Development | Designing and developing the user interface using Angular. |
| Backend Development | Developing the Python backend and application functionality. |
| Data Management | Managing the information required by the platform, including opportunity-related data. |
| Testing | Testing features, identifying errors and verifying that functionality works correctly. |
| Project Management | Organising tasks, monitoring progress and managing priorities using Agile practices. |
| Documentation | Recording project decisions, development activities and reflections. |

---

## 4. Agile Values

The development of Opportunity Connect was guided by the following Agile values:

### Collaboration

The team worked together to discuss requirements, development tasks and challenges. Collaboration was important because different aspects of the application had to work together as one system.

### Communication

Regular communication helped the team understand progress, identify problems and make decisions about the project.

### Working Software

The team focused on developing functional features rather than only producing documentation. Completed functionality was tested to ensure that it contributed to the overall product.

### Adaptability

The project requirements and development priorities could change as new information, technical challenges and user needs were identified. The Agile approach allowed the team to adapt to these changes.

---

## 5. Working Agreements

The team established the following working agreements:

1. Team members should communicate important project updates and challenges.
2. Tasks should be clearly defined before development begins.
3. Large requirements should be broken into smaller and manageable tasks.
4. Team members should keep track of their assigned work.
5. Problems should be communicated as early as possible.
6. Completed functionality should be tested before being considered finished.
7. Changes to the project should be managed using version control.
8. Team members should respect each other's ideas and contributions.
9. Project priorities should be reviewed when requirements change.
10. Important project decisions and progress should be documented.

---

Communication and collaboration were important parts of the development process.

---

## 7. Definition of Done

A task was considered **Done** when:

- The required functionality had been implemented.
- The implementation was integrated into the application.
- The functionality had been tested.
- Identified errors had been addressed.
- The feature worked according to its requirements.
- The relevant task had been updated.
- The work was ready to be demonstrated or included in the product.

The Definition of Done helped establish a common understanding of when a task could be considered complete.

# Reflection

Creating the Agile Team Charter helped me understand that successful software development involves more than writing code. It requires clear communication, defined responsibilities and an agreed approach to completing work.

The Team Charter provided a foundation for the Opportunity Connect project by establishing expectations around collaboration, task management, communication and the completion of work. It helped me understand the importance of having a shared understanding of what the team was trying to achieve.

One of the key lessons I learned was the importance of breaking a large software project into smaller and more manageable pieces of work. Opportunity Connect contains different areas of functionality, including the user interface, backend services and opportunity-related functionality. Using Agile practices made it easier to approach these areas incrementally instead of attempting to develop the entire system at once.

The Team Charter also highlighted the importance of accountability. Each team member needs to understand their responsibilities and communicate when they encounter challenges. This makes it easier to track progress and reduces the possibility of important tasks being overlooked.

Another important lesson was that software development requirements can change. As development progressed, technical challenges, feedback and user requirements could affect the project's priorities. The Agile approach allowed the team to adapt instead of treating the original plan as completely fixed.

Overall, creating and applying the Agile Team Charter improved my understanding of Agile software development and demonstrated how structured collaboration, communication and shared responsibilities can contribute to the successful development of Opportunity Connect.
